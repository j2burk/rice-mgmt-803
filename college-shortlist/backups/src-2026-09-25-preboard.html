Pick your early lane by mid-October

  • Early Decision is binding, and it ends the merit comparison. An ED admit must enroll before seeing other schools' merit offers. Use ED only for a school worth attending at full price.
  • For merit, Early Action wins. UT (Oct 15), USC (Nov 1), SMU (Nov 1) and UChicago EA (Nov 2) are all non-binding.
  • Stanford REA is compatible with that plan. Stanford allows other early applications where applying early is required for merit, which covers USC's Nov 1 deadline. Public schools (UT, UW) are always fine. For SMU EA alongside Stanford REA, confirm with Stanford first.
  • UT Austin moved Early Action to Oct 15. Test scores are required there.

Merit scholarships that need early action

  • USC: apply by Nov 1 (EA or ED) to be considered for Trustee / Presidential / Deans awards.
  • SMU: apply by Nov 1, then the President's Scholars essay by Nov 16.
  • UT Austin: Forty Acres Scholars full ride. Apply early; Scholarship Interest Form (résumé + essay) by Jan 15.

NYU is the one exception

NYU only considers applicants for its scholarships, merit included, if the CSS Profile is filed. No other school on this list needs aid forms for merit.

Common App personal essay (650 words), goes to every school except UW's own section
Across all nine applicationsThe channel already shows up in the activities list, résumé and several supplements, so the Common App essay should show something else: character, family, a turning point, life off camera. Prompts 2, 5 and 7 often work best for that. Readers across nine schools should meet three sides: the builder (channel/business), the thinker (real curiosity) and the person (values, relationships). Assign each supplement to one of the three so they don't repeat.
